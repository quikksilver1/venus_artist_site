June 29, 2026 Austin Chart Milestone

No. 3 on ReverbNation’s Austin Singer-Songwriter Chart

Venus reached No. 3 out of 49 artists on ReverbNation’s Austin Singer-Songwriter chart, climbing from No. 9 in only a few weeks. For Venus, that momentum carries special meaning: her independent music career is part of a larger goal to create the best possible future for her two autistic children.
